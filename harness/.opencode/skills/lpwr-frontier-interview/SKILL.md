# Frontier Interview

Turn a loose idea into feasible interview notes, one question at a time.

- One question per round; each round narrows on the previous answer. Never
  batch questions — a batch lets the key ambiguity hide behind easy answers.
- Steer every round toward what `lpwr-propose` needs: the outcome, the
  constraints, the non-goals. Anything else is small talk; cut it.
- Stop when all three are explicit enough to propose from. Stopping early
  produces a vague proposal; stopping late interrogates — three to five
  rounds is the normal shape.
- Record the outcome as interview notes the human feeds into `lpwr-propose`.
  Never write code during an interview.
